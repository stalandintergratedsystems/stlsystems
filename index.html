About us

Professional land solutions built on accuracy and integrity

Staland Integrated Systems is a professional land surveying and geospatial company based in Diani, Kenya, serving clients across the country and internationally. We are committed to precision, reliability, and practical solutions that protect land investments and support development.

From cadastral and boundary surveys to land administration support, fencing solutions and survey training, our team combines technical excellence with real-world field knowledge to deliver results clients can trust.
