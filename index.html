Land Surveying Excellence Built on Precision

Staland Integrated Systems is a professional land surveying and geospatial solutions company based in Diani, Kenya. With 10 years of hands-on field experience, we have built a strong reputation for delivering accurate, reliable, and comprehensive surveying services.

Our expertise spans cadastral surveying, boundary demarcation, topographical mapping, land subdivision support, professional fencing solutions, and land administration services. We combine rigorous surveying standards with modern geospatial technology—including RTK GNSS systems, Total Station instruments, and advanced GIS mapping—to ensure every project meets the highest accuracy standards.
