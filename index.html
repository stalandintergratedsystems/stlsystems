About Staland Integrated Systems

Staland Integrated Systems is a professional land surveying and geospatial solutions company based in Diani, Kenya, serving clients across the country and internationally. With 10 years of field experience, we have built a reputation for precision, integrity, and reliable delivery of land-related services.

Our expertise includes cadastral surveying, boundary identification, topographical mapping, land subdivision support, fencing solutions, and land administration services. We combine professional surveying standards with modern geospatial technology including RTK GNSS, Total Station instruments, and advanced mapping systems.
