About Staland Integrated Systems

Delivering Excellence in Land Surveying & Geospatial Solutions Since 2016

Who We Are

Staland Integrated Systems is a leading professional land surveying and geospatial solutions company based in Diani, Kwale County, Kenya. With over 10 years of field experience, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for accurate, reliable, and innovative surveying solutions across Kenya and internationally.

We specialize in combining professional surveying expertise with cutting-edge geospatial technology to deliver comprehensive solutions for boundary surveys, topographical mapping, land administration, fencing solutions, and project management. Our commitment to precision, integrity, and client satisfaction has earned us recognition among developers, real estate professionals, government agencies, and private clients.
